Discrete Torus Walk

Walking the discrete torus with (1, 1)

Start at (0, 0) in Zm × Zn and keep adding (1, 1). The path runs diagonally, wraps at every edge, and comes back to the start after lcm(m, n) steps. It lands on every point exactly when gcd(m, n) = 1: that is the Chinese Remainder Theorem, drawn.

k = 0 → (0, 0) visited 1 of 28 gcd(4, 7) = 1 · lcm = 28

Fundamental domain

The m × n grid with opposite edges identified. Leaving the right edge re-enters on the left; leaving the top re-enters at the bottom.
